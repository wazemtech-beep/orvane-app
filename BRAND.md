# Orvane Studio

Fictional architecture and interiors identity applied to the existing Aroclux
Next.js application. Existing routes, layouts, typography, photography and motion
timings remain. Internal `aroclux` names are retained as implementation namespaces.

## Identity

- Charcoal #1E2427
- Deep charcoal #111719
- Ice blue #B8D9E8
- Copper #D89B7B
- Muted mineral #A4AFB4

Content, metadata and contact placeholders: `src/data/siteConfig.ts`. Palette:
`src/styles/theme.css`. Brand layout: `src/styles/brand.css`.

Team identities, project studies, testimonials, numbers and prices are illustrative
demo content, not verified commissions or results. Existing reference imagery is
retained and is not evidence of constructed Orvane projects. The email address
hello@orvane.example and phone +1 (202) 555-0172 are fictional placeholders. Forms
remain frontend-only and indexing is disabled. Original attribution is retained.

## Generated Logo

Built-in ImageGen asset: `public/brand/orvane-mark.png`.

Prompt: One quiet architectural monogram for ORVANE. A broken circular floor-plan
O ring with an off-centre vertical plane suggesting an open doorway. Refined,
simple silhouette, predominantly ice blue #B8D9E8 with a small copper #D89B7B
inset. Intended for charcoal #1E2427, but genuinely transparent background.
One tightly framed centred symbol. No text, words, mockups, shadows, gradients,
texture, variations or tiny detail. Editable live wordmark in `BrandLogo.tsx`.
